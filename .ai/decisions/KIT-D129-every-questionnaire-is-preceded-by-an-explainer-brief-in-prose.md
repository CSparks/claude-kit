**Decision:** Chris 2026-11-02, verbatim: "IN the future, it is unacceptable to present a questionnaire without an explainer brief." No AskUserQuestion fires without a prose brief in the same reply: the situation, what was checked (with file or ticket pointers), what each option does, and the recommendation with its reason. Ids are described in plain words (KIT-D128).

**Why:** The successor-agent questionnaire went out with no context and rested on a stale premise (the broker hold, lifted 2026-10-19). Chris could not judge it.

**How to apply:** Write the brief first, verify any hold or standing condition on disk, then ask. Applies to every project.
