**Decision:** The global DECISIONS contract is reframed: a questionnaire
(AskUserQuestion) is the LAST step of a decision — for when the choice has been talked
through and the maintainer just needs the options laid out to pick. A floated idea or
question ("should we maybe X?") gets prose discussion — trade-offs, open threads —
until it converges. The anti-prose-decision rule now explicitly exempts discussion:
the bug is deciding-by-insinuation, not discussing.

**Why:** The old heading ("always a questionnaire") pushed questionnaires at exploring
maintainers — Chris got an option grid when he wanted a starting point for discussion
("that's jumping the gun", 2026-08-05). Rejected: keeping the rule and relying on
judgment — the heading itself was the instruction being followed.
