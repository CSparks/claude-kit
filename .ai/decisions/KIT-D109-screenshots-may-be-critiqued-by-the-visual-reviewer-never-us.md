**Decision:** The base rule "Visual output is NOT evidence — Claude cannot judge screenshots/renders" (user-config/CLAUDE.global.md, WORKING RULES) is replaced. Screenshots may be critiqued by the `visual-reviewer` agent (agents/visual-reviewer.md) for quality: art, composition, polish, readability, obvious breakage, each finding with a concrete fix and a severity, returned to the orchestrator to become tickets. A screenshot is never proof that geometry, layout or rendering is correct; numeric validation (dumps, lints, metrics, structured exports, invariants) stays the pass/fail gate; the maintainer judges visuals finally and his UAT overrides every finding. The composed ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md is regenerated by bootstrap.

**Why:** The old no-judgment rule dated from earlier model generations; the maintainer wants a second eye between his UAT passes while he is away, but a model's read of an image is not a reproducible measurement.

**Rejected:** a screenshot verdict that can fail a landing; a reviewer that runs only on request.
